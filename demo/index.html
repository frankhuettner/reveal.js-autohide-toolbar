reveal.js-autohide-toolbar

Move the mouse to the bottom-left corner — the toolbar appears.

Press A (or the pen button) and draw on this slide.
E eraser · Ctrl+Z undo · X clear · Esc exit.

Speaker notes work: press S or use the toolbar button.

Ink sticks to the slide

Draw something, then resize the window — the ink stays on the slide content through any letterboxing. Leave the slide and come back: it's still here. Reload: still here.

Strokes are stored per slide as ratio coordinates and persisted to localStorage.

Format-change test

Draw a circle, then switch the deck format:

The circle stays a circle — strokes remember their aspect ratio and are aspect-fit, not stretched. (Buttons also demo that taps on interactive elements never trigger slide navigation.)

Touch

On a phone or tablet: the toolbar is always visible (no hover), swipe or tap the left/right half to navigate, and the pen works with your finger or stylus.
