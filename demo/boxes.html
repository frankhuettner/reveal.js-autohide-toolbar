The boxes of a reveal deck

■reveal viewport — the whole browser window
▨slide box — config width×height, scaled to fit, letterboxed. Ink lives here.
■content box — this slide's actual content

Resize the window: green keeps its aspect ratio and floats inside orange — the gap between them is the letterbox. The numbers top-right update live.
PowerPoint, Keynote & Google Slides paint this letterbox black; reveal paints the whole viewport with the theme / slide background instead, so normally you never see it — that's why this deck outlines it.

Short slide

With center: true (the default) reveal centres the blue content box vertically inside the green slide box. The blue box is only as tall as its content — the green box is always the full slide.

config.margin

reveal reserves margin (default 0.04 = 4%) of the window around the slide box. So even when the window has exactly the slide's aspect ratio, green stays a little smaller than orange — that gap plus any letterboxing is the space around your slides.

Try Reveal.configure({ margin: 0.3 }) in the console. Scale is also clamped to maxScale (default 2) — on a large display the green box stops growing and the letterbox gets fat; raise it (maxScale: 4) to use the pixels.

Now press A

The annotation layer shows its own dashed outline while you draw — it sits exactly on the green slide box: that's the writable area, on normal slides and on board slides alike (boards keep the deck's slide format).

Ink is stored in slide-box coordinates: window size, margin and maxScale may change any time. But pick the aspect ratio (width×height) before annotating — switching 4:3 → 16:9 later stretches existing strokes relative to the content.

Who controls what?

configgeometry — width×height (the ratio), margin, minScale/maxScale
themingCSS — colours & fonts, incl. the letterbox: paint .reveal-viewport black + .slides white for the PPT look
pluginbehaviour — toolbar, ink, boards, download/print; adapts to whatever geometry and theme you pick

The main demo deck uses 16:9 + margin: 0 + maxScale: 4 (config) and black letterbox bars (theming) — this deck keeps reveal's defaults so you can see them.
